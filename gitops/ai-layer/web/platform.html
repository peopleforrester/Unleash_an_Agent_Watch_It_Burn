Why there are no clickable links for most of these. Only the console you are reading now (this page, BurritoBot and your terminal) is published through a load balancer. Grafana, Argo CD, Prometheus, Loki and Tempo are ClusterIP: they have no address reachable from your browser. Rather than print a URL that cannot work, each row gives you the port-forward that makes it reachable and the exact command for its password. Run the port-forward in your terminal, then open the localhost URL.
