## The stack at a glance (2026-09-05)

One EKS cluster per attendee, provisioned by Terraform through `fleet.sh` across five AWS accounts, sharing one lab VPC per account. Each cluster runs its own Argo CD app-of-apps (about 39 applications on the attendee profile) that reconciles from the `staging` branch. On it: the platform (Istio ambient, cert-manager, External Secrets, Kyverno with a minimal policy floor, KubeArmor, Falco with Talon and Falcosidekick, NetworkPolicy), observability (Datadog operator and agent, OpenTelemetry operator and collector, plus the Grafana stack of Prometheus, Loki, Alloy and Tempo), and the AI layer: the kagent-hosted BurritoBot agent on Amazon Bedrock (Nova Pro by default) reached over a private VPC endpoint, its real tools served by `workshop-mcp` through agentgateway, a deliberately poisoned `evil-mcp`, and the guard-proxy in front of the model carrying LLM Guard input and output scanning, a budget cap and the cost meter. The student surface is the console: BurritoBot, the lab page with eight attack-then-fix challenges, and a web terminal. Around the fleet: the provisioning app (Railway, Flask, SQLite on a volume) that hands out clusters, the apex router (Caddy on Railway) that maps `<name>.agenticburn.com` to each cluster's console load balancer, the rounds deck, and the feedback form. Since 2026-09-05 every cluster reports its own name to Datadog, and attendee clusters ship their telemetry to the student's org and to the instructor org at once, so the instructor screen sees the whole room.
