What the figure is:
  * Each account's billed usage (Cost Explorer record type Usage, unblended) on the window's UTC days. It is
    measured, not estimated, and it is the whole account: a co-tenant's spend on the same days is inside it.
  * Only the accounts listed above. A fleet account left off the command line is not in it.
  * An account marked as read from recorded replies was not asked by this run: its figures come from the
    saved replies of the same daily queries, made in that account by someone holding its credentials, and
    the source named under it says who and when.
  * Tax, credits and refunds are left out. Tax is posted on the bill's own days, mostly the 1st of the month,
    not on the usage days it is for.
  * The tag-filtered total is 0.00 because only the payer account can activate cost-allocation tags, and the
    payer is the lab-account provider's, which is not asked (#392). It is printed so the day the tags are
    active, the per-delivery figure is in this report without a code change.
